Where should your datasets live?

Pick a drive with plenty of free space (an external SSD is ideal). NeuroRadVault creates a tidy folder there: one folder per disease domain, sorted by priority, with a shortcut to every dataset's official page. You can move to another drive later.

Tip: a folder that already has a vault in it is simply reopened, with your downloads intact.
